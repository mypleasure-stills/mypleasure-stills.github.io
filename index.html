Mypleasure Stills 平面影像紀錄

Mypleasure Stills 提供展覽、活動與正式場合的平面攝影，涵蓋展位、人物側拍、貴賓與外賓交流、論壇與會議。

活動第一天即交付精修照片，第 2–3 天可追加拍攝或調整。照片可直接用於新聞稿、官網、社群與存檔。

平面與影像團隊同場作業，具備中英雙語的專業溝通能力，可直接與外賓、國際媒體與跨國企業對接。

Mypleasure Stills provides photography for exhibitions, events and formal occasions: booth coverage, portraits and candids, delegations and international guests, forums and conferences.

On multi-day events we deliver retouched selects on the first day, and days two and three stay open for added shots or corrections. Photos come ready for press releases, websites, social media and archives.

Our stills and video teams work side by side, with professional communication in both English and Mandarin, ready to work directly with international guests, media and multinational clients.
